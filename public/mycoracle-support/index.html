Frequently Asked Questions

How do I draw a card?

From the reading interface, choose a one-card or three-card reading. Follow the on-screen prompts to shuffle, select, and reveal your card or cards.

Where are my journal entries saved?

Your journal text is saved in MycOracle's local app storage on your device. It is not account-synced or backed up to the cloud by MycOracle. Removing the app or clearing its app data may remove your entries.

How do I adjust text and accessibility settings?

Open Settings, then choose Accessibility. You can select a default, red-green, blue-yellow, or high-contrast color mode, use Atkinson Hyperlegible text with adjusted spacing, and enable larger text.

Why is the music or sound not playing?

Check the Music and Sound FX controls in MycOracle Settings, then check your device volume. On supported devices, also confirm that silent mode is not preventing playback.

Can I use MycOracle without an internet connection?

Yes. Card readings, saved journal entries, history, favorites, accessibility controls, music, and sound effects use bundled or on-device resources and remain available offline. Internet access is needed to open public support, privacy, or statistics pages and to send any optional aggregate activity events when that service is configured.

How can I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email support@quantumnonsense.com with a short description. For bugs, device and app-version details are especially useful.
